Training Disciplines

Structured programs for every starting point.

Whether you are learning foundational barbell mechanics or preparing for an endurance event, every session is coached with clear progressions.

Strength Training

Barbell & Progressive Strength

Who it suits: Lifters looking to build compound strength, joint resilience, and confident technique.

What a session involves: Structured warm-ups, primary compound lifts (squat, hinge, press, pull), and targeted accessory work paced for proper recovery.

Personal Training

1-on-1 Individualized Coaching

Who it suits: Individuals seeking tailored programming, return-to-training guidance, or dedicated accountability.

What a session involves: Custom movement screening, bespoke strength and conditioning blocks, and real-time biomechanical feedback.

Group Classes

Vanguard Team Training

Who it suits: Members who thrive on shared energy, structured coaching, and balanced full-body workouts.

What a session involves: Capped at 14 athletes, combining kettlebell mechanics, sled work, and ergometer intervals with scalable options.

Conditioning

Aerobic & Threshold Engine

Who it suits: Runners, cyclists, hybrid athletes, and anyone looking to improve cardiovascular stamina and work capacity.

What a session involves: Paced intervals across Concept2 rowers, SkiErgs, assault bikes, and turf carries using heart-rate or RPE zones.

Yoga & Mobility

Athletic Mobility & Breathwork

Who it suits: Desk-bound professionals, heavy lifters, and athletes looking to expand active range of motion.

What a session involves: Loaded stretching, controlled articular rotations, Vinyasa flow sequences, and parasympathetic down-regulation.

Beginner Program

Foundations Onboarding

Who it suits: Newcomers to strength training or members returning after a long break away from the gym.

What a session involves: Step-by-step instruction on fundamental movement patterns, equipment setup, and pacing in a supportive setting.

The Club

Thoughtfully zoned spaces built for focus.

Natural daylight, acoustic baffling, and generous spacing between racks mean you never have to wait on equipment or fight for floor space.

Strength & Cardio Floor

Equipped with 12 half-racks, competition-grade barbells, calibrated bumper plates, dumbbells up to 150 lbs, and a dedicated endurance mezzanine featuring Concept2 ergometers, Woodway treadmills, and stair climbers.

Functional Training Zone

A 40-yard shock-absorbing indoor turf track outfitted with weighted prowler sleds, competition kettlebells, sandbags, plyometric boxes, and gymnastics rigs for athletic conditioning.

Movement & Mobility Studio

Acoustic-insulated studio with sprung oak flooring, natural light, bolsters, and blocks for yoga, breathwork, and joint mobility classes.

Recovery Suite

Dedicated post-training recovery featuring cedar dry saunas, cold plunge tubs, and a quiet lounge stocked with percussion therapy tools.

Locker & Changing Rooms

Private rain-shower suites, keypad day-use lockers, fresh towel service, grooming stations, and filtered bottle-fill stations throughout.
